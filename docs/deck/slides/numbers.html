Here is why this is hard. In our synthetic dataset, three systems describe the same work, invoice matching, and give three different answers: timesheets say 71.5 hours a week, the ticket system says 19.5, and the activity log measures 32.9 hours of hands-on time. AgentOffice does not pick one silently. The World Model stores all three with their provenance and flags the disagreement as a conflict. The Conflict Critic then explains which source to trust: the activity log for sizing an automation. The data is synthetic and clearly labelled.
