Office teams lose hours to work nobody has time to fix

Who

Small office teams

Ops, finance and admin teams with no automation engineer on staff.

Where it hurts

Repeated manual steps

The same spreadsheet steps every day, across tools nobody connected. In the Gulf, one sheet mixes Arabic and Western digits.

What’s missing

Someone to fix it safely

Find the routine, prove it’s worth it, build and test it, and get sign-off.

Our user is a small office team: operations, finance or admin, with nobody whose job is automation. Their time goes into the same handful of manual steps, many times a day, spread across email, PDFs, spreadsheets and an ERP. In the UAE and Saudi Arabia the same sheet can hold one staff ID in Western and in Arabic-Indic digits, so a plain lookup quietly misses rows. Plenty of tools can automate a task once you know which task. What these teams lack is someone to find the routine, prove it is worth automating, build it, test it, and get it approved safely.
