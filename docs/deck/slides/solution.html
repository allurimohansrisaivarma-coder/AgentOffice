The product is one pipeline with three stages, and each stage answers one of the three track challenges. Discover finds where the time goes across fragmented sources. Explain says why, with every number cited. Decide and Build, our primary track, picks what to automate, builds it, tests it and asks a human to approve. Thirteen agents share one World Model: every agent reads its context from it and writes back what it learns. H-MEM, a second engine, carries lessons and human decisions between runs.
